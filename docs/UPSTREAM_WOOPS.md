# WOOPS provenance

Daniel R. Martin developed WOOPS in Dmitry V. Matyushov’s group at Arizona State University. Martin’s 2015 user guide, *Painting the Protein with Water Orientations Surrounding Amino Acids*, describes the PSF, beta-tagged PDB, and DCD workflow for mapping hydration-water orientational order onto a protein surface in VMD.

See [Martin and Matyushov, *Dipolar Nanodomains in Protein Hydration Shells*, J. Phys. Chem. Lett. **6**, 407–412 (2015)](https://doi.org/10.1021/jz5025433) and the [source distribution notice](../DISTRIBUTION_NOTICE.md).
